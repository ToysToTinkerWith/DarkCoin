# Discord announcement draft

Copy the post below into Discord:

```text
⚔️ The DarkCoin Playground is open for public testing!

Bring your DarkCoin Champion NFT into the 3D arena, try out its weapons and magic, practice on the strike dummy, and battle other champions.

🔗 https://dark-coin.com/arena/playground

Connect your wallet, pick a champion, review its 3D preview and stats, then enter the arena.

🔐 No funds are transferred to enter or play. The playground checks your champion ownership using public blockchain data; it does not ask you to sign a blockchain transaction or deposit your tokens or NFTs. Your assets stay in your wallet. There are no wagers or on-chain rewards in this test.

🧪 This is an early public test. Expect bugs, balance changes, and occasional resets while we improve combat, animations, and multiplayer performance.

🎮 Controls
• WASD — move
• Shift — run
• Mouse — aim
• Right click — draw / stow your weapon
• Left click — attack
• E — cast equipped trait magic

Please share feedback and clips of anything that feels off, especially hit detection, weapon animations, or lag. Let us know which champion and weapon you were using!
```
